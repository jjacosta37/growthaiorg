# Compliance Guidelines

These rules apply to everything Sift drafts for {{ project_name }}. They're a starting template, not legal advice: review them with counsel and edit to fit your jurisdiction and registration status.

## Hard rules
1. **No performance claims or promises.** Never state or imply that using {{ project_name }} will produce returns, beat the market, reduce losses, or improve outcomes. Don't cite past performance of any strategy, portfolio, or signal.
2. **No personalized investment advice.** Don't tell anyone what to buy, sell, or hold, or how to allocate their money, even when a Reddit post asks directly. Explain concepts, trade-offs, and questions to consider, and suggest consulting a licensed professional for personal decisions.
3. **Educational framing.** Present financial topics as general education. Use "one way to think about…" and "investors often consider…", not "you should…".
4. **No disparaging competitors.** Compare factually and fairly, or don't compare at all.
5. **No fabrication.** No invented statistics, studies, quotes, testimonials, user numbers, or features. If a figure is used, it must come from a named, verifiable source.
6. **Disclose affiliation.** Whenever {{ project_name }} is mentioned in a community setting (Reddit, forums, replies on X), disclose that the author is its founder.

## Phrases to avoid
- "Guaranteed", "risk-free", "can't lose", "sure thing", "beat the market", "outperform"
- "You should buy/sell…", "this stock will…", "the best investment"
- Anything implying {{ project_name }} is endorsed by a regulator or financial institution

## Safer alternatives
- "helps you analyze…" instead of "helps you profit from…"
- "a tool for researching…" instead of "a tool for winning…"
- "past performance doesn't indicate future results" whenever historical data is discussed

## Required disclaimers
- **Blog posts:** end with "This article is for educational purposes only and is not financial advice."
- **Reddit comments that mention {{ project_name }}:** include a short disclosure, such as "(Disclosure: I'm the founder of {{ project_name }}.)"

## Before posting, check
- [ ] No claims about returns or performance
- [ ] No personal advice to a specific person
- [ ] Every fact and number is sourced or removed
- [ ] Competitors described fairly, if at all
- [ ] Founder disclosure present where {{ project_name }} is mentioned
